# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Keep contributions practical, respectful, and focused on making local music
generation easier and safer for users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Sharing private tokens, credentials, or gated model files.
- Encouraging license bypasses or model redistribution.
- Submitting generated audio that infringes on third-party rights.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or revoke access when needed to
protect the project and its users.
